# Changelog

## 2026-11-07: buyer safety and flow
- **Daily spend cap:** new setting `spendCapUsdDay` (`PUT /api/settings`, Payments → Daily spend cap; `null` = no cap, `0` blocks every paid route; negative, non-finite or non-numeric values are `400`). A paid route is skipped when the last 24 hours of ledger spend, plus the worst case of requests still in flight, plus this request's worst case (`promptEstimate` / `outCap`, price × 1.05) would pass the cap; free and local routes are never limited, and a skipped paid route fails over to them. If every route is skipped for the cap the answer is `402 spend_cap_reached` with how to raise it. `GET /api/status` has `spendCap: {usdDay, spentUsd}`. Tests: `router/test/spendcap.test.mjs`.
- **Alerts on Home and Payments:** non-blocking banners derived from live state, each with the one action that fixes it: "No ETH for gas" (faucet link and address copy), "Escrow below $1" (add funds), "You used 80% of today's spend cap" and "Daily spend cap reached" (raise the cap). No new polling.
- **Guided funding on Payments:** a three-step "Fund this node" stepper (gas from a faucet, USDG: mint on the MOCK testnet token or fund from your wallet, deposit into the escrow), each step done or next with its button, collapsing to one line when complete. The advanced escrow form is unchanged below it.
- **Back up this node (Connect):** what lives in the data directory and what losing it costs, its path, whether secrets are encrypted at rest, a checklist, and a download of the public facts only (node id, wallet address, escrow). `GET /api/status` gains `node.dataDir` and `node.encrypted` (a boolean; never a key or the passphrase).

Uptime lowers a peer's reputation score; both appear in the public market ("first token 412 ms · up 97%") and in `/api/models` and `/api/peers`. Fixes a peer losing its probe history when it was re-added.
- **Relay (sell from behind a home router):** `ELYRA_RELAY_URL` (or `--relay`) makes a node dial out to a relay and be reachable at `<relay>/r/<id>` with no port forwarding; `ELYRA_RELAY=1` turns a public node into a relay.
